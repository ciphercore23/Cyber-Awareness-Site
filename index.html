Cyber Safety Awareness

Protecting students from online threats.

What is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting devices, networks, and personal information from digital attacks.

Common Online Threats

  • Phishing emails
  • Weak passwords
  • Fake websites
  • Social engineering scams

Password Safety Tips

  • Use long passwords
  • Include symbols and numbers
  • Do not reuse passwords
  • Avoid sharing passwords

Why I Built This

I created this beginner cybersecurity awareness website to help students learn how to stay safe online and understand common cyber threats. I have seen my schools service getting attacked several times and students getting strange links to which it leads to being hacked and loosing all their files. By making this website I am sure those basic knowledge on how they can be safe online will be benefited.
